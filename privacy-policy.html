Privacy Policy

App Name: Gears & Gloss - Invoice Generator  |  Package ID: com.gearsandgloss.garage  |  Effective Date: September 24, 2026
Google Play Privacy & Data Safety Summary:

Gears & Gloss - Invoice Generator prioritizes user privacy. All business data, customer records, and invoice documents created within the application are stored locally on your device. We do not sell or rent your personal or business data to third parties.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Gears & Gloss - Invoice Generator ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our mobile application and website. By using Gears & Gloss, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

To provide you with garage management, GST invoice generation, and job card tracking features, the app collects and processes the following information:

  • User & Account Profile: Account owner's name, email address, and login status.
  • Company & Business Setup: Business name, business type, address, city, state, postal code, phone number, company email, website, GSTIN, PAN, bank account details (Bank Name, Account Number, IFSC, UPI ID), default tax rates, custom invoice prefixes, logo paths, and digital signature images.
  • Customer & Vehicle Records: Customer name, company name, phone number, email address, billing/shipping address, customer GSTIN, vehicle registration number, vehicle make and model, and odometer reading.
  • Job Cards & Invoice Documents: Document numbers, dates, line items (parts and labour categories, quantities, rates, tax calculations), discounts, advance payments, payment methods, payment status (Paid/Unpaid/Partial), job card vehicle entry status, complaints list, and assigned technician names.

3. App Permissions & Contact Access

To enhance convenience while generating invoices, the application requests the following Android runtime permission:

  • READ_CONTACTS Permission (android.permission.READ_CONTACTS):
    When you tap the contact picker button on the customer entry form, the app requests permission to view your device's contacts solely to allow you to select an existing contact's name and phone number to auto-fill customer billing fields.
    Note: Contact information is accessed strictly on demand upon your explicit action. Your phonebook contacts are never uploaded to any cloud server or transmitted to third parties.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to provide and improve app functionality, including:

  • Generating professional A4 PDF GST invoices, quotations, estimates, cash receipts, and job cards.
  • Managing vehicle service histories and customer contact directories.
  • Embedding UPI payment details and QR links into invoice documents for convenient bill collection.
  • Displaying financial summaries and dashboard statistics within the app.

5. Data Storage, Security & Offline-First Policy

Gears & Gloss operates on an offline-first architecture. All business profiles, customer contacts, invoice logs, job cards, and signature assets are stored locally within your Android device's isolated application storage sandbox using encrypted SharedPreferences.

Because your data resides on your device, you maintain primary ownership and control over your data. We implement standard security measures within the Android operating system to guard against unauthorized local access.

6. Third-Party Sharing & Advertising

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data, customer databases, or financial records with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks.

7. User Rights & Account / Data Deletion

In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies regarding account and data deletion:

  • In-App Data Purge: You can permanently delete all local business profiles, invoices, and customer logs by navigating to Android Settings > Apps > Gears & Gloss > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Web Account Deletion Portal: You can submit an online account/data removal request at any time using our Account Deletion Facility Page. Requests submitted through the web portal are processed promptly by our support team.

8. Children's Privacy

Gears & Gloss - Invoice Generator is designed for business and professional use by automotive garage owners and managers. The app is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
