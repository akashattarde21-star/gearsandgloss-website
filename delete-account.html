How Account Deletion Works

Gears & Gloss is designed with a privacy-first architecture where primary user profile details, company GSTIN/PAN info, signature files, customer lists, and job card logs are stored locally in your device's secure app sandbox.

  • Immediate In-App Deletion: You can purge all local account data instantly by clearing the app data in Android Settings > Apps > Gears & Gloss > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data, or by uninstalling the application.
  • Web Account Support Request: Fill out the official form below to request account/data removal assistance from our support team.
